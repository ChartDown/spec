      Syntax cheat sheet
      
    
        
    ## Name
    Start a section
    
        
    > MD: text
    Music director note
    
        
    1 | 4 | 5 | 1
    Bars, separated by |
    
        
    1 4 | 6m 5
    Chords share a bar evenly
    
        
    1:3 4:1
    Uneven split, in beats
    
        
    1/3  5sus
    Slash and suspended chords
    
        
    <1>  =
    Hold a bar; continue it
    
        
    '4  ''4  1!  1~
    Push, short push, stab, fermata
    
        
    x  x:3  %
    No chord; partial rest; repeat the last bar
    
        
    [p:2] 5 | 1
    Pickup bar, then full bars
    
        
    R[ 1 3 4 5 ]
    Unison riff in single notes
    
        
    {mf}  {drums in}
    Dynamic or cue
    
        
    "short words"
    Lyric landmark
    
        
    |: ... :| 3x
    Repeat; [1] and [2] for endings
    
        
    {2x drums: in}
    Cue for one pass of a repeat
    
        
    {segno}  {D.S. al coda}
    Navigation: also {coda}, {to coda}, {fine}, D.C.
    
        
    @meter 6/8  [m:2/4]
    Meter change; one bar in another meter
    
        
    @tempo 72  {rit}
    Tempo change; rit., accel., a tempo
    
        
    @use Chorus
    Reuse an earlier section
    
        
    > MD drums: text
    MD note for one instrument
    
        
    {drums: in}
    Cue for one instrument, on a bar
    
        
    @key E
    Key change from here on
